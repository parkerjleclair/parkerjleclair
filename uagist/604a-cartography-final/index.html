GIST 604A – Cartography Final

Simplified Bedrock Geology of Adirondack Park, New York

Project Overview

This final project for GIST 604A Cartography presents a simplified bedrock geologic map of Adirondack Park in northern New York. The goal was to preserve the major geologic patterns of the region while reducing the complexity of the original geologic mapping so the final map would remain clear and readable at a regional scale.
